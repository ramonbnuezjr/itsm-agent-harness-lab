# Email Delivery Troubleshooting

Use this procedure for delayed, rejected, or missing email.

1. Determine whether the issue affects one message, one mailbox, one domain, or many users.
2. Capture the sender, recipient, approximate timestamp, and non-sensitive portion of any rejection code.
3. Check the published mail-service status and the user's quarantine folder.
4. Do not ask users to forward confidential message content into the ticket.
5. Escalate suspected service-wide outages to messaging operations and suspected phishing to security operations.

For a single rejected message, preserve the rejection code and route details needed by messaging operations.
