# Incident Priority and Escalation

Assign priority from both impact and urgency; do not infer priority from urgency alone.

- P1: A critical service is unavailable to most users, public safety is at risk, or essential operations cannot continue. Page the incident commander immediately.
- P2: A major service is degraded or a department cannot perform time-sensitive work. Notify the responsible support group promptly.
- P3: A limited set of users is affected and a workaround exists. Route through the normal support queue.
- P4: A minor issue or informational request with little operational impact. Handle through the standard request process.

When impact is unclear, collect affected-user count, service scope, workaround availability, and time sensitivity before changing priority.
